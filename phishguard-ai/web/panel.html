Performance at a glance

ROC curves

X: false-positive rate · Y: true-positive rate. Dashed diagonal: chance ranking.

What the forest uses

Top ten features, summed across their one-hot categories. Impurity importance is model-specific and not causal.

What mistakes cost

False negative: a phishing record passes as legitimate. In deployment, this could leave a user exposed.

False positive: a legitimate record is flagged. Excessive blocking creates friction and erodes trust.

One fixed group-held-out split is an experiment, not production validation. No confidence interval, live threat feed, domain holdout or temporal holdout is claimed.
