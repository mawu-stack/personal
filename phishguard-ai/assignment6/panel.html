DDOS GUARD / METHOD SELECTION
Detect floods without excessive false alarms.
Problem & research question
DDoS attacks threaten availability. A useful detector must recognize attack traffic while limiting disruption to legitimate users.
How do a compact decision tree and a class-balanced Random Forest compare on attack recall, F1 and false-positive rate using the same eight traffic features?
The Random Forest is a proposed comparison, not an assumed improvement. The measured results determine which trade-offs it offers.
Fair comparison
Use the Kaggle mirror's named training/testing files. Fit median imputation on training only. Remove conflicting training feature patterns and duplicate patterns. Exclude test feature patterns overlapping retained training. Fix seed 42 and all model settings before evaluation.
This traditional-ML comparison establishes a baseline. It does not claim a novel algorithm or satisfy a deep-learning contribution by itself. A stronger follow-up needs validation-based method development and an independent external or temporal evaluation.
