    Recommended next steps ↓

    Your flow checks.

    Saved only in this browser on this device. No automatic upload. Exported histories contain only the measurements you chose to save.

    Protect availability.

    If traffic looks suspicious

    Confirm the pattern against normal traffic, server metrics and multiple time windows. Check whether a legitimate event or health check explains the increase. Preserve relevant logs and escalate to your network or hosting team.

    If service is disrupted

    Contact your hosting provider or incident-response team. Use approved upstream DDoS mitigation, CDN/WAF protections and rate controls. Monitor legitimate-user impact while changing controls.

    Read the result carefully

    One flow cannot establish a coordinated flood. An attack-like prediction is a triage signal, not proof. A benign-like prediction cannot rule out an attack. Compare across time, destinations and protocols before taking action.

    Safe lab boundaries

    This workspace analyzes existing measurements. It never sends floods, probes a target or blocks real users.
