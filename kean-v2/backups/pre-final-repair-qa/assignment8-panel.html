      Detection thresholds
      • At least 5 failures per IP in 60 seconds.
      • At least 4 distinct usernames per IP in the selected event set.
      • Successful sign-in after at least 3 failures in the prior 60 seconds.
      • Administrator login outside 08:00–18:00 UTC.
      • At least 30 requests per IP in 10 seconds.
      • SQL-like boolean bypass or UNION SELECT pattern. Script tags and inline event-handler patterns flag XSS.

        Limitations and Privacy

        The assistant uses approved actions and fixed parameter schemas. It has no OS or file access. Simulated traffic and product listings are classroom examples. Answers may be wrong. The microphone starts only after opt-in. BYOK keys and the six-turn conversation are held for the current session. With a provider connected, relevant questions and recent conversation context may go to that provider. Keep passwords and payment information out of the command box.
