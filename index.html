              Modern NIST password recommendations emphasize length and passphrase complexity rather than arbitrary frequent expiration periods.
            

            

              

                Legacy

Security Reflection: What Should I Share Online?

          Reflection
        


        

          

            When posting or communicating on the internet, a great guideline to follow is that you should only share what you feel comfortable with the entire world knowing. You should never share sensitive personal information, including your home address, personal phone number, passwords, financial details, or student ID numbers.
          

          

            Oversharing sensitive personal information creates dangerous security and privacy risks, allowing attackers to conduct social engineering, launch targeted phishing attempts, or attempt identity theft.
